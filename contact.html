Response Times

We aim to respond to all inquiries within:

  • Support requests: 24-48 hours
  • Privacy/GDPR requests: 30 days (as required by law)
  • Urgent security issues: 12-24 hours

For account deletion requests, please visit gosshive.app/delete-account or use the in-app deletion feature for immediate processing.
