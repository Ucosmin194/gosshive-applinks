Delete Your GossHive Account

If you want to delete your account and personal data, use the in-app deletion flow below.

In-app deletion (fastest option):

  • Open GossHive
  • Go to Settings > Privacy & Data
  • Select Delete Account
  • Type DELETE to confirm

Your account is deleted immediately after confirmation.

Can't access the app?

Email us and we will help you complete the request.

Email: privacy@gosshive.app

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and authentication record
  • Your posts, comments, messages, and notification tokens
  • Content that must remain for integrity is anonymized where feasible

Backups may retain deleted data for up to 90 days.
