Delete Your GossHive Account

If you want to delete your account and personal data, use the in-app deletion flow below.

In-app deletion (fastest option):

  • Open GossHive
  • Tap the Settings icon (gear icon in top navigation)
  • Scroll to find "Delete Account" button
  • Type DELETE to confirm (paste is disabled for security)

Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately after confirmation.

Can't access the app?

Email us and we will help you complete the request.

Email: privacy@gosshive.app

What gets deleted immediately

We permanently delete all your data, including:

  • Account data: Profile, email, authentication credentials
  • User content: All posts, comments, and direct messages
  • Engagement data: Votes, comment likes, and anonymous aliases
  • Social data: Blocks, reports (submitted by you and about you)
  • System data: Notifications, conversation participants, and metadata
  • Media files: Profile pictures and uploaded images
  • Moderation records: Flagged content entries and suspensions

Note: Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 90 days, after which it is permanently removed.
